I'm a **PhD candidate in Statistics and Machine Learning** at **Amsterdam UMC and VU Amsterdam**. I build tools that bring statistical methods closer to the questions people want to answer.

**My interests:** causal inference, statistical modelling, machine learning, and research software.

### What I'm building

**[Causal Capybara](https://github.com/HYHBalci/causal-capybara)** is a desktop studio for applied causal inference. Start with a research question, explore your study design, inspect diagnostics, and compare estimates with R and Python.

[Explore the project](https://github.com/HYHBalci/causal-capybara#readme) · [See the releases](https://github.com/HYHBalci/causal-capybara/releases)

I also keep public forks of [stochtree](https://github.com/HYHBalci/stochtree), for stochastic tree ensembles, and [ThinkInteractionsTreat](https://github.com/HYHBalci/ThinkInteractionsTreat), for regression with linked shrinkage and treatment effects.
